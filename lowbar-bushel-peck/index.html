About

Lowbar Coffee (Ryan Barlow) and Bushel & Peck Bakery (Peyton Agliata) share a neighborhood cafe on NW 5th Ave, with design by Vivi Lowery. Skate-friendly vibe, painted skateboards and checkered floors.

Find us on Instagram @bushelandpeck.fl · Instagram @lowbar.coffee · Facebook.
