The name

A "Lor" is someone from Lorestan, a province in the Zagros mountains of Iran. Lor Kebab is an homage to that culinary culture. Chef Mohammadali Asgari cooked in Persian restaurants from New York to Miami before opening here in December 2025.

"For example, the koobideh is an art."

Mohammadali Asgari, in Mainstreet Daily News
